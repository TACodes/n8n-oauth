Privacy policy

This app is a private, single-user automation. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Gmail account.

What data is accessed

The app reads emails that the owner has labelled KnowledgeBase/inbox, marks them as read and adds a KnowledgeBase/imported label. It can also send failure notifications to the owner's own email address.

How data is used and stored

Email content is copied only into the owner's self-hosted Trilium Notes instance. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or any other purpose.
